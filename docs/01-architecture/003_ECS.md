# 33. Event Design Rules

The Risk, Decision, Policy, Execution, and Portfolio domains SHALL satisfy the following constraints:

- Every Decision SHALL be preceded by a RiskCalculated event.
- Every ExecutionRequested event SHALL originate from a PolicyPassed event.
- Every PositionOpened SHALL reference its originating OrderSubmitted event.
- Every PositionClosed SHALL preserve the complete trade lifecycle.
- Portfolio metrics SHALL be updated exclusively through execution events.
- EmergencyTradingStopTriggered SHALL take precedence over all automated execution flows.
- Event replay SHALL reconstruct the complete trading history without altering historical records.

# Chapter Summary

This chapter defines the event contracts governing the autonomous trading lifecycle, including:

- Risk Assessment
- Decision Generation
- Policy Validation
- Execution
- Orders
- Deals
- Positions
- Portfolio Management
- Failure Events

These events form the execution backbone of Veerox ATI, ensuring that every automated trading action is deterministic, auditable, replayable, and governed by explicit business events before interacting with external trading infrastructure.
