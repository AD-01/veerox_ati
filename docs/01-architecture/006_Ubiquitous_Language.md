# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the official business vocabulary for the Veerox Autonomous Trading Intelligence Platform (ATI).

All stakeholders—including product managers, software architects, developers, QA engineers, DevOps engineers, AI agents, technical writers, and automated tooling—SHALL use the terminology defined in this document.

A business term SHALL have one meaning only.

Multiple terms SHALL NOT be used for the same business concept.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Ubiquitous Language SHALL:

- Eliminate ambiguity.
- Standardize engineering terminology.
- Synchronize business and technical language.
- Reduce documentation inconsistencies.
- Improve AI-assisted implementation accuracy.

---

# 3. Naming Principles

The following principles SHALL apply.

### Rule 1

One business concept SHALL have one official name.

---

### Rule 2

A technical implementation SHALL inherit the business terminology whenever possible.

Example

Business:

```
Strategy
```

Implementation:

```
StrategyService
StrategyRepository
StrategyCreatedEvent
```

NOT

```
TradingLogicManager
AlgorithmExecutor
```

---

### Rule 3

Names SHALL be singular.

Correct

```
Strategy
Trade
Portfolio
```

Incorrect

```
Strategies
Trades
Portfolios
```

---

### Rule 4

Abbreviations SHALL be avoided unless industry standard.

Accepted:

- API
- EA
- MFA
- MT5
- UUID

---

# 4. Business Glossary

## Account

A broker trading account connected to Veerox ATI.

---

## Active Strategy

The strategy currently selected by the Strategy Orchestrator for execution.

---

## Aggregate

The transactional consistency boundary defined by Domain-Driven Design.

---

## Audit Record

An immutable record describing an important system event.

---

## Broker

An external trading platform capable of receiving execution instructions.

---

## Confidence Score

A normalized value representing the confidence of a trading decision.

Range:

```
0.00 – 1.00
```

---

## Connector

A software component responsible for communicating with an external execution platform.

Example:

- MT5 Connector

A Connector SHALL NEVER contain business logic.

---

## Decision

A validated business outcome produced by the Decision Domain.

Examples:

- Execute Trade
- Reject Trade
- Pause Strategy
- Close Position

---

## Domain

An independent business capability with clear ownership.

---

## Domain Event

An immutable record describing something that has already happened.

Example

```
TradeOpened
```

---

## Expert Advisor

An executable trading plugin implementing one or more trading strategies.

Expert Advisors SHALL NOT make business decisions.

---

## Execution

The act of submitting a validated trading instruction to a broker.

---

## Indicator

A mathematical calculation used to describe market behavior.

Indicators SHALL provide information only.

---

## License

A commercial authorization allowing a customer to access a product or capability.

---

## Liquidity

The ability of the market to absorb trading volume with minimal price impact.

---

## Market Health

A composite evaluation of current market quality.

---

## Market Regime

The classified state of the market.

Possible values include:

- Trending
- Ranging
- Breakout
- High Volatility
- Low Volatility
- Recovery
- Uncertain

---

## Market Snapshot

A complete representation of current market conditions generated by the Market Intelligence Domain.

---

## Notification

A message delivered to a user through an approved communication channel.

---

## Plugin

A deployable software extension providing additional functionality without modifying the platform core.

---

## Policy

A mandatory business rule determining whether an action is permitted.

---

## Portfolio

A logical collection of one or more managed trading accounts.

---

## Portfolio Exposure

Total risk currently allocated across all active positions.

---

## Position

A currently active market trade.

---

## Position Size

The volume allocated to a trade after risk validation.

---

## Risk

Potential financial loss associated with a trading decision.

---

## Risk Score

A normalized measurement representing current trading risk.

Range:

```
0.00 – 100.00
```

---

## Session

A recognized financial market trading session.

Examples:

- Sydney
- Tokyo
- London
- New York

---

## Strategy

A business-level trading methodology.

A Strategy SHALL define trading logic.

It SHALL NOT execute trades directly.

---

## Strategy Orchestrator

The domain responsible for selecting, coordinating, activating, suspending, and replacing strategies.

---

## Strategy Transition

The controlled replacement of one active strategy with another after policy validation.

---

## Trade

A completed execution accepted by an external broker.

---

## Trade Signal

A potential trading opportunity produced by a strategy before policy validation.

---

## Trading Decision

The final business decision generated by the Decision Domain after all evaluations are complete.

---

## Workspace

An isolated operational environment within an organization.

---

# 7. Event Naming Convention

Domain Events SHALL use the following format:

```
<Entity><PastTenseVerb>
```

Examples:

```
TradeOpened
TradeClosed
StrategyActivated
StrategyPaused
LicenseValidated
MarketSnapshotUpdated
```

---

# 8. Command Naming Convention

Commands SHALL use imperative verbs.

Examples:

```
ActivateStrategy
PauseStrategy
ValidatePolicy
ExecuteTrade
ClosePosition
CreateWorkspace
AssignLicense
```

---

# 9. Query Naming Convention

Queries SHALL begin with "Get", "Find", "Search", or "List".

Examples:

```
GetPortfolio
GetTrade
GetStrategy
FindWorkspace
ListAccounts
```

---

# 10. Repository Naming Convention

Repositories SHALL follow:

```
<Entity>Repository
```

Examples:

```
StrategyRepository
TradeRepository
PortfolioRepository
LicenseRepository
```

---

# 11. Service Naming Convention

Application services SHALL follow:

```
<Entity>Service
```

Examples:

```
StrategyService
ExecutionService
NotificationService
MarketplaceService
```

---

# 12. API Naming Convention

REST endpoints SHALL use plural nouns.

Examples:

```
/users
/workspaces
/strategies
/trades
/portfolios
/licenses
```

Actions SHALL use sub-resources.

Examples:

```
POST /strategies/{id}/activate

POST /strategies/{id}/pause

POST /trades/{id}/close
```

---

# 13. Database Naming Convention

Tables SHALL use singular PascalCase or snake_case consistently (repository-wide convention to be finalized in the Database Architecture document).

Primary keys:

```
id
```

Foreign keys:

```
strategy_id
workspace_id
portfolio_id
```

Timestamps:

```
created_at
updated_at
deleted_at
```

---

# 14. AI Implementation Rules

AI-generated code SHALL use the terminology defined within this document.

AI tools SHALL NOT invent alternative names for existing business concepts.

---

# 15. Success Criteria

This document SHALL be considered complete when:

- Naming conventions are standardized.
- Reserved terminology is established.
- AI implementation ambiguity is eliminated.
