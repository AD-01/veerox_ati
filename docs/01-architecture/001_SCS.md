# 18. Autonomous Trading Pipeline

```text id="autonomous-pipeline"
Market Intelligence
        │
        ▼
AI Service
        │
        ▼
Strategy Orchestrator
        │
        ▼
Strategy Service
        │
        ▼
Expert Advisor Service
        │
        ▼
Risk Service
        │
        ▼
Decision Service
        │
        ▼
Policy Service
        │
        ▼
Execution Service
        │
        ▼
Portfolio Service
```

Each service owns its domain, communicates through versioned events, and SHALL NOT directly access another service's persistence layer.

---

# 19. Dependency Rules

- Strategy Service SHALL NOT call the Execution database.
- Risk Service SHALL remain independent of AI implementation.
- Decision Service SHALL consume published events rather than querying downstream services.
- Execution Service SHALL only process approved decisions.
- Portfolio Service SHALL derive state from execution events.
- Manual overrides SHALL be handled by the Strategy Orchestrator before decision generation.
- Every service SHALL expose only documented public contracts.

---

# Chapter Summary

This chapter defines the service contracts for the core autonomous trading pipeline, including:

- Expert Advisor Service
- Strategy Orchestrator Service
- Risk Service
- Decision Service
- Policy Service
- Execution Service
- Portfolio Service

Together these services implement the deterministic decision-making and execution flow that powers Veerox ATI while preserving domain isolation, event-driven communication, and enterprise-grade maintainability.
