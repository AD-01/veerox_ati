# 37. Risk Management Schema

Schema

```text id="risk-schema"
risk
```

The Risk Domain is the highest-priority business domain after Identity.

**No trade SHALL reach the Execution Domain without passing through the Risk Domain.**

The Risk schema SHALL manage:

- Capital Protection
- Position Sizing
- Portfolio Exposure
- Daily Risk
- Drawdown Control
- Correlation Analysis
- Margin Analysis
- Risk Policies

# 42. Design Rules

The Risk, Portfolio, Decision, and Policy schemas SHALL satisfy the following constraints:

- Every Decision SHALL reference the Risk Assessment that produced it.
- Every Decision SHALL be evaluated by one or more Policies.
- Every Policy evaluation SHALL be immutable.
- Portfolio statistics SHALL be derived from execution data, not manually edited.
- Risk calculations SHALL never overwrite historical assessments.
- Decision explanations SHALL remain reproducible using recorded inputs.
- Every rejected Decision SHALL preserve the rejection reason.
