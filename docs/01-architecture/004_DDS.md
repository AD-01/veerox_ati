# 31. Strategy Schema

Schema

```text id="strategy-schema"
strategy
```

The Strategy schema defines the business representation of trading strategies.

A Strategy is a logical trading methodology independent of any implementation language.

Examples:

- Smart Money Concepts
- ICT
- Grid
- Scalping
- Trend Following
- Mean Reversion
- Breakout
- Arbitrage
- News Trading

A Strategy SHALL NOT contain executable code.

Executable implementations SHALL reside in the Expert Advisor schema.

# 32. Expert Advisor Schema

Schema

```text id="ea-schema"
expert_advisor
```

The Expert Advisor schema stores executable trading engines.

Every EA SHALL implement one or more Strategies.

One Strategy MAY have multiple EA implementations.

Example:

```text id="ea-example"
Strategy

↓

Trend Following

↓

EA V1

↓

EA V2

↓

AI Optimized EA

↓

Institutional EA
```

# 36. Design Rules

The Strategy and Expert Advisor schemas SHALL satisfy the following constraints:

- A Strategy SHALL remain independent of executable code.
- Multiple EAs MAY implement the same Strategy.
- Strategy selection SHALL precede EA selection.
- Automatic switching SHALL preserve historical decisions.
- Manual overrides SHALL take precedence over AI recommendations until expiration.
- Every strategy change SHALL be auditable.
- Every backtest SHALL remain reproducible using recorded versions and parameters.
