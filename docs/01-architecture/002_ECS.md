# 19. Market Intelligence Events

**Publishing Service**

```text
market-intelligence-service
```

The Market Intelligence Domain SHALL publish events whenever market conditions, news, sentiment, or economic data materially change.

These events SHALL NOT initiate trade execution directly.

---

- Risk Service

# 24. Event Flow

```text
Market Snapshot Generated
            │
            ▼
Market Regime Changed
            │
            ▼
AI Recommendation Generated
            │
            ▼
Strategy Ranking Generated
            │
            ▼
Strategy Selected
            │
            ▼
EA Selected
            │
            ▼
Decision Generation
```

The flow SHALL remain deterministic and replayable.

---

# 25. Event Design Rules

The Market, AI, Strategy, and EA domains SHALL satisfy the following rules:

- Market events SHALL never execute trades.
- AI recommendations SHALL remain advisory.
- Strategy selection SHALL always precede EA selection.
- Every automatic strategy switch SHALL be recorded.
- Manual overrides SHALL generate independent events.
- Event consumers SHALL remain idempotent.
- Every event SHALL be replay-safe.

# Chapter Summary

This chapter defines the event contracts for:

- Market Intelligence
- News & Economic Calendar
- AI Recommendations
- Strategy Lifecycle
- Expert Advisor Lifecycle
- Strategy Orchestrator
- Automatic Strategy Switching
- Backtesting

These events provide the intelligence pipeline of Veerox ATI, transforming market observations into strategy and EA selections while preserving complete traceability and separation between analysis, recommendation, and execution.
