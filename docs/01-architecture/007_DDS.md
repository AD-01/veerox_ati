# 49. AI Schema

Schema

```text id="ai-schema"
ai
```

The AI schema provides the complete data foundation for artificial intelligence, machine learning, recommendation systems, explainability, and continuous model governance.

**Important Principle**

The AI schema SHALL NEVER become the system of record for trading decisions.

The Decision Domain SHALL remain the only authoritative source for execution decisions.

# 54. Design Rules

The AI, Marketplace, Licensing, and Billing schemas SHALL satisfy the following constraints:

- AI models SHALL be version controlled.
- Every prediction SHALL reference the model version that produced it.
- Marketplace assets SHALL be cryptographically verifiable.
- Every download SHALL require a valid license.
- Licenses SHALL support multiple controlled activations.
- Billing records SHALL remain immutable after successful payment.
- Training datasets SHALL remain reproducible through stored versions and checksums.
- Marketplace products SHALL support future expansion without schema redesign.
