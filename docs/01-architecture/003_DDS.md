# 25. Market Intelligence Schema

Schema

```text id="market-schema"
market
```

The Market schema is responsible for collecting, normalizing, enriching, and storing all market intelligence consumed by the Decision Engine.

This schema SHALL NOT contain trading decisions.

It SHALL contain only market observations and analytical outputs.

# 29. Data Retention Rules

The Market schema SHALL implement different retention policies.

| Table | Retention |
|---------|-----------|
| market_ticks | 30 Days (configurable) |
| market_candles | Permanent |
| market_snapshots | Permanent |
| technical_indicators | 180 Days |
| news_articles | Permanent |
| news_sentiment | Permanent |
| calendar_events | Permanent |
| market_scores | Permanent |

Historical analytical data SHALL support AI training and backtesting.

---

# 30. Design Rules

The Market Intelligence schema SHALL satisfy the following constraints:

- Raw provider data SHALL remain immutable.
- Normalized data SHALL be generated by the Market Intelligence Domain.
- Duplicate news SHALL be clustered before analysis.
- Calendar revisions SHALL preserve history.
- AI recommendations SHALL remain advisory only.
- Market intelligence SHALL NEVER directly trigger trade execution.
