# 20. Connector Service

**Service Name**

```text id="connector-service"
connector-service
```

---

## Responsibilities

The Connector Service is responsible for communication between the Veerox Platform and Veerox Agent.

It SHALL:

- Register Agents
- Authenticate Agents
- Maintain Heartbeats
- Synchronize Accounts
- Synchronize Orders
- Synchronize Positions
- Synchronize Deals
- Deliver EA Packages
- Execute Trading Commands

---

## Public APIs

```text id="connector-api"
POST /agents/register

POST /agents/heartbeat

POST /agents/accounts/sync

POST /agents/orders/sync

POST /agents/positions/sync

POST /agents/deals/sync

POST /agents/trades/execute
```

# 29. Cross-Service Rules

Every service SHALL comply with the following architectural rules:

1. A service SHALL own its database schema exclusively.
2. Cross-service database queries SHALL NOT be permitted.
3. Communication SHALL occur through:
   - REST APIs (synchronous)
   - Domain Events (asynchronous)
4. Public APIs SHALL remain backward compatible within a major version.
5. Commands SHALL modify state.
6. Queries SHALL never modify state.
7. Domain Events SHALL be immutable.
8. Every service SHALL expose Health, Metrics, and Version endpoints.
9. Every outbound event SHALL include Correlation ID and Causation ID.
10. Business logic SHALL remain inside the owning service's Domain Layer.
